# Third party grammars

The syntax grammars in `src-tauri/grammars` come from the built-in language extensions of
[VS Code](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode) (MIT license, Copyright (c) 2015 - present Microsoft Corporation).
Most of them were written in other projects, named in the `version` line below. See
`extensions/cgmanifest.json` in the vscode repository for their licenses.
